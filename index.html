      Settings

      OpenRouteService key

      Free, no card, from openrouteservice.org. Without one the app falls back to a straight line between your points, which is fine for trying things out. The key is stored on this device only.

      Display

      Makes everything bigger for reading at arm's length. If the whole app looks tiny, check that Chrome's Desktop site option is switched off — that shrinks every page to about a third size.
